# Prompt 3 – Updating your portfolio later

Your portfolio should grow with you. Each time you finish a new lab, earn a certification or start a new job:

1. Open your design again: claude.ai → **Artifacts** (left sidebar) → your portfolio design, or open the chat where you built it.
2. Paste the matching prompt below.
3. Export again and redeploy to the **same** Netlify project ([Step 6](../docs/06-update-your-site.md)). Your link stays the same.

**New project**
```
Add a new project card at the top of the Projects section:
Name: <name>
Problem: <one line>
What I built: <one or two lines>
Tech: <comma separated>
Result: <one line>
Link: <GitHub URL>
Keep everything else exactly as it is.
```

**New certification**
```
Add this certification to the Certifications section: <name>, <issuer>, <year>,
link: <verification URL>. Keep everything else exactly as it is.
```

**New job**
```
Add this role to the top of the Experience timeline:
Company: <company>
Role: <title>
Dates: <start> - Present
What I do: <one or two lines>
Also update my title in the hero to "<new title>". Keep everything else exactly as it is.
```

> 💡 Always end with **"Keep everything else exactly as it is."** Otherwise Claude may redesign parts you were happy with.
