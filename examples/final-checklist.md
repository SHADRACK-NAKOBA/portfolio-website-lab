# Final checklist – before you share your link

Tick every box. Open your **live link** (not the canvas) on your **computer and your phone**.

## Content
- [ ] My name, title and photo are correct.
- [ ] The tagline says clearly what I do, in one line.
- [ ] At least 3 projects, each with **Problem → What I built → Result**.
- [ ] Every project has a working **GitHub** (or demo) link to **my own** repository.
- [ ] Skills only include things I can discuss in an interview.
- [ ] Certifications listed are ones I have **actually earned**.
- [ ] No placeholder text ("Your Name", "Lorem ipsum", `example.com`).
- [ ] No spelling mistakes in my name, companies, schools or certifications.
- [ ] No private details (home address, date of birth, personal phone number).

## Links
- [ ] Email button opens an email to **my** address.
- [ ] LinkedIn button opens **my** profile.
- [ ] GitHub button opens **my** profile.
- [ ] Resume button (if any) downloads my latest CV.

## Quality
- [ ] Looks good on my phone (nothing cut off or overlapping).
- [ ] The address bar shows the padlock 🔒 (HTTPS).
- [ ] The link has a professional name (`firstname-lastname.netlify.app`).
- [ ] LinkedIn Post Inspector shows my name and title ([Step 8.2](../docs/08-make-it-stand-out.md#82-check-the-linkedin-link-preview)).

## Shared
- [ ] LinkedIn: Contact info, Featured section and About.
- [ ] CV header (clickable link).
- [ ] Email signature.
- [ ] GitHub profile Website field.
- [ ] Posted on LinkedIn and tagged Prince.

✅ All ticked? You're ready. Send your link to recruiters with confidence.
