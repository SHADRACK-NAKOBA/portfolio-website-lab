# Step 7 – Share it everywhere

[⬅ Previous: Update your site later](06-update-your-site.md) | [Next: Make it stand out ➡](08-make-it-stand-out.md)

A portfolio only helps if recruiters see it. Put your link in **all** of these places.

## 7.1 LinkedIn – Contact info
1. Go to https://www.linkedin.com → click **Me** → **View Profile**.
2. Under your name and location, click **Contact info**.
3. Click the **pencil ✏️** icon.
4. Under **Website**, click **+ Add website**.
5. Paste your link (`https://your-name.netlify.app`) and set **Website type** to **Portfolio**.
6. Click **Save**.

## 7.2 LinkedIn – Featured section (most visible)
1. On your profile, click **Add profile section** (or **Add section**) → **Recommended** → **Add featured**.
2. Click **+** → **Add a link**.
3. Paste your portfolio link → **Add**.
4. Title: `My Portfolio – <Your Name>`; description: one line, for example *"AI & cloud projects with live GitHub code."* → **Save**.

## 7.3 LinkedIn – About section
End your **About** text with:
> *See my projects and code: https://your-name.netlify.app*

## 7.4 LinkedIn – announce it
Create a post with a screenshot of your portfolio and the link. Tag **Prince** so it can be shared further. (Use the [Prince LinkedIn post prompt](08-make-it-stand-out.md#86-optional-announce-it-with-a-great-linkedin-post).)

## 7.5 Your CV / resume
Add the link in the **header**, next to your email and LinkedIn:
```
Jordan Lee | jordan.lee@example.com | linkedin.com/in/... | your-name.netlify.app
```
Make it a **clickable** link in Word/Google Docs: select the text → **Ctrl + K** → paste the full `https://` link.

## 7.6 Email signature
- **Gmail:** ⚙️ **Settings → See all settings → General → Signature → Create new** → add the link → **Save Changes** (bottom of the page).
- **Outlook:** **Settings → Mail → Compose and reply → Email signature** → add the link → **Save**.

## 7.7 GitHub profile
1. Go to https://github.com → your profile picture → **Your profile** → **Edit profile**.
2. Paste your link into **Website** → **Save**.

## 7.8 Job applications
Paste the link into every application's **Website / Portfolio** field, and mention it in cover letters: *"You can see my projects at ..."*
