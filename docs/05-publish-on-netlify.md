# Step 5 – Publish on Netlify (free)

[⬅ Previous: Export the HTML](04-export-html.md) | [Next: Update your site later ➡](06-update-your-site.md)

Netlify hosts your site for free, with HTTPS (the padlock 🔒), at a link like `https://your-name.netlify.app`. On the free plan the site stays online as long as your account is active.

## 5.1 Create a free Netlify account
1. Go to https://app.netlify.com/signup.
2. Click **Sign up with GitHub** (recommended), Google, or email.
3. Answer the short welcome questions (any answers are fine) and skip any "import from Git" or team-invite screens.

## 5.2 Deploy your folder
1. Go to **https://app.netlify.com/drop** (or scroll to the bottom of your Netlify dashboard and find the **drag and drop** area).
2. Open File Explorer (Windows) or Finder (macOS) next to the browser.
3. **Drag the whole `my-portfolio` folder** (not just the file) onto the drop area.
4. Wait 10–30 seconds. Netlify shows a **Preview URL** like `https://candid-dango-cada58.netlify.app`.
5. Click the link. Your portfolio is live on the internet. 🎉

> Make sure you're **logged in** when you drop the folder. A site dropped without an account must be **claimed** to stay online. If Netlify asks you to claim it, click **Claim** and sign in.

## 5.3 Give it a professional name
`candid-dango-cada58` doesn't look professional. Change it:
1. On your project's page in Netlify, find the **Preview URL** and click **Customize** below it.
   *(Alternatively: **Project configuration** (or **Site configuration**) → **General** → **Project details** → **Change project name**.)*
2. Type a name using your name, for example **`firstname-lastname`**, then click **Save**.
   - Lowercase letters, numbers and hyphens only.
   - If it's taken, try `firstname-lastname-ai` or `firstnamelastname`.
3. Your new link is **`https://firstname-lastname.netlify.app`**. Open it to check.

📝 **Write your link down.** You'll use it everywhere in [Step 7](07-share-it-everywhere.md).

> **Shortcut:** Claude Design's **Export** menu can also **send the design to Netlify** directly. The drag-and-drop method above is shown because it works the same for everyone and makes later updates easy.

## Check before you continue
- [ ] `https://your-name.netlify.app` opens your portfolio on your computer **and on your phone**.
- [ ] The padlock 🔒 shows in the address bar.
