# Step 2 – Build it in Claude Design

[⬅ Previous: Prepare your content](01-prepare-your-content.md) | [Next: Refine your design ➡](03-refine-your-design.md)

**Claude Design** turns your description and files into a visual design on a canvas next to the chat. It's available on **all Claude plans, including Free**.

> ℹ️ **Usage limits:** Claude Design uses the same usage limit as the rest of your Claude account. One complete, well-prepared first message (the brief) gives the best result for the least usage. If you hit the limit, you'll have to wait until it resets.

## 2.1 Sign in to Claude
1. Go to https://claude.ai.
2. Sign in, or click **Sign up** and create a free account (Google sign-in or email).
3. Use a computer (not a phone) for this lab. The canvas is much easier to use on a big screen.

## 2.2 Start a design
1. Click **New chat** (top left).
2. In the message box at the bottom, click **Output** and choose **Design**.
   > Don't see **Output**? Look for a **+** or **tools / settings** icon in the message box, or just start your message with *"Create a design:"*. See [Troubleshooting T2](09-troubleshooting.md#t2-i-cant-find-output--design).

## 2.3 Attach your files
1. Click the **paperclip / +** icon in the message box → **Upload a file** (or drag the files into the message box).
2. Select **both**:
   - `Portfolio_Data.xlsx`
   - `profile.jpg`
3. Wait until both files show as attached (small file cards above the message box).

## 2.4 Paste the brief
1. Open [`prompts/01-portfolio-brief.md`](../prompts/01-portfolio-brief.md) (on GitHub or in a text editor).
2. Copy **everything inside the grey box**.
3. Paste it into the message box.
4. Change the lines marked `<< CHANGE >>`:
   - style (for example `modern dark`),
   - accent colour (for example `deep blue #2348C5`, or `choose one that fits`),
   - the optional extra request (or delete that line).
5. Click **Send** (the arrow button).

## 2.5 Watch it build
- Claude reads your spreadsheet and builds the site on a **canvas** beside the chat. It takes 1–3 minutes.
- When it finishes, read Claude's message: it lists anything from your spreadsheet it **couldn't use**. Fix those in [Step 3](03-refine-your-design.md).
- Scroll through the whole canvas and check that your **name, photo, projects and links** are all there.

> ❌ **Photo missing, placeholder text, or invented skills?** These are the most common problems. See [Troubleshooting](09-troubleshooting.md#t3-my-photo-doesnt-show).

## Check before you continue
- [ ] Your portfolio is on the canvas with your real name and photo.
- [ ] Your projects appear with their links.
